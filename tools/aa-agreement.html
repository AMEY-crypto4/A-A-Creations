Independent contractor status

Provider is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, or agent of Client. Provider is responsible for its own taxes, insurance, and business expenses. Nothing in this Agreement creates a joint venture or employment relationship between the parties.

06

Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public business, technical, and financial information confidential, and will use it only to perform this Agreement. This obligation survives completion or termination of this Agreement and does not apply to information that is independently known, becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, or must be disclosed by law.

07

Ownership & intellectual property

Upon receipt of full payment, all rights to the final deliverable transfer to Client. Until full payment is received, all work product remains the property of Provider. Provider retains the right to reference and display the completed work in its own portfolio unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. Each party keeps ownership of any pre-existing tools, code, or materials it brings into the project.

08

Warranty disclaimer

Provider will perform the work in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards. Except as stated in this Agreement, the deliverable is provided "as is," without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose beyond the agreed scope.
