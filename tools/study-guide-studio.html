Turning this into income

Two ways to run it: sell study materials, or use it to tutor faster.

Tutoring  If you tutor, this cuts prep time dramatically — lesson plans and quizzes in minutes instead of an hour. Charge your normal tutoring rate, prep faster, take more students.

Always verify  Spot-check generated facts against the real textbook before selling or teaching from it — treat this as a fast first draft, not a finished, fact-checked product.
