## Outreach templates — first contact

**WhatsApp — business HAS a website (worst-score leads)**
> Hi [Business Name] 👋 I came across your website and noticed [specific issue from Signals Found — e.g. "it doesn't load great on mobile"]. I'm Amey, I help local businesses get a fast, modern site. I'd like to send you a free 2-minute video walking through exactly what's costing you customers right now — no obligation at all. Want me to send it over?

**WhatsApp — "no website found" leads**
> Hi [Business Name] 👋 I couldn't find a website for you online — just your [Instagram/Google listing]. I build clean, professional websites for local businesses starting at ₹7,999, done in about a week. Want me to send a quick example of what yours could look like?

**WhatsApp — "Get Found Everywhere" pitch (no website, platform-onboarding angle)**
> Hi [Business Name] 👋 Noticed you're not listed on [Zomato/Swiggy/Practo/Urban Company — pick from Suggested Platforms column] yet — that's usually where customers are searching for a [restaurant/salon/service] like yours first. I can help get you set up there this week, no website needed yet. Want me to walk you through it?

**Follow-up nudge (2-3 days, no reply)**
> Hi [Name], just following up on my note above — happy to send that free audit whenever's convenient, no pressure either way!

**Email version (slightly more formal)**
> Subject: Quick note about [Business Name]'s website
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I help local businesses get modern, mobile-friendly websites — I took a quick look at [Business Name]'s site and noticed [specific issue]. I'd like to send over a short free audit video showing exactly what I found, no strings attached.

> Would that be useful? Happy to send it today.

**In-person opener** (often your highest-converting channel)
> "Hi, quick question — do you handle your own website, or would you be open to a free 2-minute review of it? I help local shops get better, faster sites — no pressure, just wanted to show you what I noticed."

## After they say yes

1. Send the free audit (video or written, pulling straight from the lead-finder "Signals Found" column)
2. Send the **Proposal** (`proposal-template.html`) — personalized "What We Found" + matching package recommendation
3. On approval, send the **Quote** (from the pricing workbook) → collect 50% deposit
4. Build → preview link → revisions → launch
5. Send the **Invoice** for final payment
6. Ask for a testimonial + referral the same day you launch
